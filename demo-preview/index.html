The Heritage Story

A Handcrafted Haven in Galle's Historic Citadel

Originally built during the Dutch-colonial era of maritime trade, Villa Galle Fort has been meticulously restored into an ultra-exclusive private sanctuary.

High timber-beam ceilings, authentic Sri Lankan jackwood antiques, and hand-poured encaustic tiles echo centuries of island history. Step through our arched doorway into a serene oasis of central lotus courtyards, frangipani blossoms, and the whisper of sea breezes.

Every detail is curated for the discerning traveler: from your complimentary morning feast of hopper breakfasts to evening sunset cocktails served on the rooftop verandah overlooking the ramparts.
